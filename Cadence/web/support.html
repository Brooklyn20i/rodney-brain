Cadence

Support

Cadence is currently available to invited workspace users.

Account access

If you cannot sign in, first confirm that you are using the email address attached to your Cadence invitation. Passwords and two-factor codes should never be sent to support.

Workspace or data questions

Contact your Cadence workspace administrator through the support channel supplied with your invitation. Include the affected screen, approximate time and a description of what happened. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other secrets.

App troubleshooting

  • Confirm the iPhone has a working internet connection.
  • Close and reopen Cadence, then use Retry if a recoverable network error appears.
  • If the session has expired, sign in again.
  • For persistent problems, provide the app version, build number, iPhone model and iOS version to your workspace administrator.

Privacy and deletion requests

Use the same workspace support channel for access, correction or deletion requests. See the Cadence privacy policy for details.

Contact

Your workspace invitation identifies the administrator or support channel responsible for your account. App Review enquiries are handled through the contact details maintained in App Store Connect.
