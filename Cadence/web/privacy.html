Cadence

Privacy policy

Effective 20 September 2026

Cadence is a private workspace application that helps authorised users review commitments, delegated work and decisions. This policy covers the Cadence native iPhone app.

Data we process

  • Account information: email address and a service-generated user identifier.
  • Workspace content: work items, decisions and related content that you or authorised workspace members provide.
  • Authentication data: session credentials required to keep you signed in. The iPhone app stores its session in Apple Keychain.

How we use data

We use this information only to authenticate you, enforce workspace access, display your executive brief and carry out actions you request. The native app does not contain advertising and does not use data for tracking.

Storage and disclosure

Cadence stores workspace data in its hosted production service. Access is limited by account and workspace permissions. We do not sell personal data. Service providers may process data only to operate the service, subject to their security and privacy obligations.

Retention and deletion

Workspace data is retained while the workspace account is active or as required for legitimate operational, security or legal purposes. To request access, correction or deletion, contact your Cadence workspace administrator through the support channel supplied with your invitation.

Security

The app uses standard HTTPS/TLS in transit and Apple Keychain for the local session. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but access controls and server-side authorisation are used to protect workspace data.

Children

Cadence is intended for organisational users and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support channel supplied with your workspace invitation or contact your Cadence workspace administrator. You can also review the Cadence support page.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
